About Me

I am an AI and data science researcher working at the intersection of machine learning, biomedical informatics, and healthcare. My work focuses on developing computational approaches that transform complex clinical data into useful and interpretable information.

My current research involves large language models, natural language processing, electronic health record data, clinical phenotyping, and predictive modeling. I work with both structured clinical data and unstructured clinical text to study clinically meaningful outcomes and develop scalable AI-based methods for healthcare research.

My background combines engineering, data science, and biomedical informatics. I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering and subsequently completed graduate training in Applied Data Science. This multidisciplinary background has shaped my approach to solving complex healthcare problems using statistical modeling, machine learning, and modern AI methods.
