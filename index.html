About Me

I am an AI and data science researcher working at the intersection of machine learning, biomedical informatics, and healthcare. My work focuses on developing computational approaches that transform complex clinical data into useful and interpretable information.

My current research focuses on large language models, clinical natural language processing, clinical phenotyping, and predictive modeling. I use structured and unstructured information from electronic health records and other real-world clinical data sources to study clinically meaningful outcomes and develop scalable AI-based methods for healthcare research.

I hold a Master's degree in Applied Data Science. My training in data science, quantitative methods, and engineering has shaped my approach to solving complex healthcare problems using statistical modeling, machine learning, and modern AI methods.
